WhistleZim processes report content, messages, optional contact details, workflow records, and minimal security records to operate a reporting channel. Anonymous reporting does not require direct identity data. Pseudonymised records remain protected personal data. Absolute anonymity is not promised because content, devices, networks, and infrastructure may identify a person. Controller roles, lawful bases, transfers, rights handling, and final retention terms require documented review before real intake.
