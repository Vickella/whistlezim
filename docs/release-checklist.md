# Controlled release checklist

- [ ] Provider terms and intended commercial/non-commercial classification approved.
- [ ] Zimbabwe legal duties, controller roles, transfers and GDPR scope reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Real contacts and accurate controller/provider notices published.
- [ ] Independent threat/security review has no critical/high findings.
- [ ] MFA, RLS, conflicts, encryption, logs and recovery attack paths pass.
- [ ] Encrypted backup, key escrow and isolated full restore drill pass.
- [ ] Attachments remain disabled or pass quarantine/scan/backup review.
- [ ] Monitoring, incident contact and escalation are staffed.
- [ ] Organisation control and independent handler are verified.
- [ ] Capacity/pause/retention risks are accepted.
- [ ] Open-source licence is selected after owner/licence review.
- [ ] Owner signs and dates launch approval.

Failure of any item means demo mode, synthetic data and closed intake.
